🇬🇭 Ghana Speed Cameras Guide

The Ghana Police Service (MTTD) has deployed automated radar speed cameras across major Greater Accra transit links:

📍 Monitored Locations & Limits:

  • Borteyman Sports Facility Corridor: 80 km/h
  • N1 Highway (Fiesta Royale Hotel): 80 km/h
  • N1 Highway (Baah / Bar Junction): 80 km/h
  • Kawukudi (Olusegun Obasanjo Highway): 50 km/h
  • Kanda Highway: 50 km/h

🛡️ Road Safety Tips:

  • Approaching Alert: SpeedCheck warns you 5 km/h before reaching the limit to allow smooth braking.
  • Urban Centers: The default speed limit in Accra town areas is 50 km/h unless otherwise posted.
  • School & Hospital Feeder Roads: Strictly 30 km/h.

Privacy Policy

SpeedCheck is 100% private and ad-free.


1. Location Data

Your GPS coordinates are used exclusively inside your browser to calculate current speed and distance to approaching speed camera zones. No location data is ever transmitted, logged, or uploaded to any server.

2. Zero Tracking & No Ads

SpeedCheck does not use cookies, advertising identifiers, or analytics trackers. Everything runs client-side on your device.

3. Offline & Standalone

All core detection and voice algorithms function entirely offline once cached.
